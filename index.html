FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a cab with Shreya Travels in Vadodara?

Call +91 9664734841 or send a WhatsApp message with your pickup point, drop location, date and preferred vehicle. We will confirm availability and the fare.

What is the cab fare from Vadodara to Ahmedabad Airport?

The approximate fare is ₹1,800 - ₹2,200 for a sedan, ₹2,800 - ₹3,200 for an SUV and ₹4,200 - ₹4,600 for an Innova Crysta. The final fare depends on the vehicle and timing, and is confirmed when you book.

Do you provide one-way outstation cabs?

Yes. We offer one-way drops from Vadodara to cities like Ahmedabad, Surat, Navsari, Mumbai, Rajkot and Somnath. Ask us for the one-way fare for your route.

Which vehicles are available?

We have AC and Non-AC cars: Swift Dzire and Etios sedans, Ertiga and XL6 SUVs, Innova Crysta, and 12 to 20 seater Tempo Travellers for groups.

Are toll and parking charges included in the fare?

This depends on the trip type. Please ask when booking, and we will tell you clearly what is included so there are no surprises on the day.

Can I book a cab at night or very early in the morning?

Yes. Our booking desk is open 24 hours a day, so you can arrange late-night and early-morning airport drops. Booking in advance is always best.

Do you offer a cab for the Statue of Unity?

Yes. We run same-day and multi-day trips from Vadodara to the Statue of Unity in a sedan, Ertiga or Innova Crysta. Send us your date and group size for a quote.
